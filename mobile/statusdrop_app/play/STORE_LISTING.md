## Full description

StatusDrop prepares high-quality video clips for WhatsApp Status using your Android phone.

Choose up to three videos or up to ten photos, pick a quality, and let StatusDrop compress and split them locally. Your originals stay on your device. Only finished clips are uploaded temporarily for verification and user-initiated WhatsApp delivery.

Features:

• On-device FFmpeg/x264 video processing
• 1080×1920 HD clips up to 29 seconds
• 1080×1920 with no splitting: one clip up to 90 seconds (needs fast internet)
• 720×1280 longer clips up to 59 seconds
• Photos to Status videos: each photo becomes a 5-second HD clip
• Automatic splitting and size checks
• Secure finished-clip upload and integrity verification
• No account or login
• No advertising or analytics SDK in the Android app
• System picker for videos and photos: no broad storage permission
• Accessible light and dark interface
• "Before you start" setup card and a step-by-step HD posting guide
• Open-source Android client
