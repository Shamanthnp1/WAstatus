## Full description

StatusDrop prepares high-quality video clips for WhatsApp Status using your Android phone.

Choose up to three videos, select 1080p or longer 720p parts, and let StatusDrop compress and split them locally. Your original videos stay on your device. Only finished clips are uploaded temporarily for verification and user-initiated WhatsApp delivery.

Features:

• On-device FFmpeg/x264 video processing
• 1080×1920 HD clips up to 29 seconds
• 720×1280 longer clips up to 59 seconds
• Automatic splitting and size checks
• Secure finished-clip upload and integrity verification
• No account or login
• No advertising or analytics SDK in the Android app
• System video picker—no broad storage permission
• Accessible light and dark interface
• Built-in 9-step HD posting guide
• Open-source Android client
