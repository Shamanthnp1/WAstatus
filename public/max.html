StatusDrop MAX

Everything in StatusDrop, plus delivery from your own WhatsApp number. Nothing uploads to StatusDrop.

Download MAX (APK) How to install

Android 10 or newer, 64-bit (ARM64) phones. About 110 MB. Installs next to the Google Play version.

What MAX adds

The Google Play version sends your finished clips from a StatusDrop WhatsApp number. MAX adds two ways to send them from a number you own, straight from your phone.

  • Method 1

    Message yourself

    Link your own WhatsApp once with a code. Your phone sends the clips to your "Message yourself" chat. Save them, then post to Status.

  • Method 2

    Your second number as a bot

    Link a second WhatsApp number you own. Send it the code from your main WhatsApp and it replies with your clips, just like the StatusDrop number.

  • Both editions

    The same compression

    1080p HD in 29-second parts, 1080p with no splitting up to 90 seconds, 720p longer parts, and photos as 5-second clips.

How to install

  1. Download the APK on your phone. Tap "Download MAX (APK)" above in Chrome or your phone's browser.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. You can turn this off again afterwards.
  3. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may offer to scan the app first. That is normal for apps from outside Google Play.
  4. Open StatusDrop MAX and pick a delivery method. Tap the 3-dot menu, then Delivery method. The app's guide shows every step with pictures.

Your number, your responsibility

The Message yourself and bot methods use an unofficial WhatsApp connection. WhatsApp may log out or restrict numbers that behave like bots, and brand-new numbers are the most at risk. Your WhatsApp login stays only on your phone. StatusDrop is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

MAX does not update itself. When a new version is out, download it here again and install it over the old one. Source code and release notes: GitHub releases.
