WhatsApp Status
video compressor

A free Android app that cuts your video into 29-second parts on your phone and sends them to your WhatsApp in HD.

Get it on Google Play

Free, no login, Android 10 or newer. Your original video never leaves your phone.

How the app works

  1. First, pick up to 3 videos.

  2. Next, your phone compresses them.

  3. Then the finished parts upload.

  4. Last, send the code to WhatsApp.

How long is your video?

2 min 10 s
Quality

5 parts

Your video’s length divided by 29 seconds, rounded up. Each part stays under 16 MB, and one delivery holds up to 20 parts.

Post your video in full HD

The bot sends your parts into a WhatsApp chat. Post each one this way, or WhatsApp compresses it again on the way to your Status.

  1. Activate on WhatsApp
  2. Send the code
  3. Read and download
  4. Open a chat and Gallery
  5. Select your video
  6. Add a caption and send
  7. Forward the new video
  8. Choose My Status and send
  9. Done, Status posted

Frequently asked questions

Blurry video

WhatsApp compresses videos to save bandwidth, and that destroys quality. StatusDrop prepares your video to the exact specs WhatsApp expects, so it stays in HD when you post it as a Status.

Long videos

StatusDrop splits a long video into 29-second parts, or 59-second parts in 720p, and sends them all to your WhatsApp for free. Post them in order and they play one after another. For videos up to 90 seconds, choose 1080p with no splitting to get a single clip (it needs fast internet).

Price

Yes, 100% free. No login, no account, no watermark and no hidden fees. Choose up to 3 videos, 300 MB in total, and get them back in HD on WhatsApp.

Formats

MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, 3GP and WMV, up to 300 MB in total. You can also pick up to 10 photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC): each one becomes a 5-second HD video clip.

Privacy

Yes. Your original video never leaves your phone. Only the finished parts are uploaded, and they are deleted from our servers as soon as they reach your WhatsApp, or within minutes if you never send the code. We never share your videos.

The website

It has been retired. Compressing videos on our servers was expensive to run, so the free Android app now does the work on your phone. That keeps StatusDrop free and sustainable.

Get the Android app

Install it from Google Play, pick a video, and the finished parts arrive in your WhatsApp. More than 10,000 videos processed so far.
