👋 Introduction

Welcome to StatusDrop (also known as WAStatus). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our video compression service.

🔒 Short version: We temporarily process your videos to deliver them to you via WhatsApp. We never sell your data. Files are automatically deleted within 5 minutes.

📋 What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our service:

  • Video files you upload for compression — stored temporarily for maximum 5 minutes
  • WhatsApp phone number — only used to deliver your compressed video back to you
  • IP address — used only for rate limiting (max 50 requests per 24 hours per IP) to prevent abuse
  • Website usage data — Google Analytics is active on this site and may process page views, interactions, approximate location, and browser or device details so we can understand and improve the service

🚫 What We Never Collect

  • Your name or personal identity
  • Email address
  • Payment information
  • WhatsApp contacts or messages other than the activation code you send us
  • Your video content for analytics or advertising
  • We do not sell your personal information

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Compressing and splitting your video into WhatsApp-compatible clips
  • Temporarily storing compressed files on Cloudflare R2 (deleted in 5 minutes)
  • Delivering compressed videos to your WhatsApp number from our own WhatsApp account
  • Preventing abuse through rate limiting
  • Measuring website usage and diagnosing problems through Google Analytics
  • When advertising is enabled, displaying and measuring ads through Google AdSense

🗄️ Data Storage & Deletion

We take data minimization seriously. Here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • Uploaded videos are stored on our server only during processing (typically 1-3 minutes)
  • Compressed videos are uploaded to Cloudflare R2 cloud storage
  • All files are permanently deleted within 5 minutes of your activation code being generated
  • If you never use your activation code, files are still automatically deleted after 5 minutes
  • We do NOT maintain any database of users or video history

🗑️ Your videos are stored for a maximum of 5 minutes — then permanently deleted from all our servers automatically.

💬 WhatsApp & Meta

We deliver your compressed video from our own WhatsApp account to yours, using an automated client built on WhatsApp's multi-device protocol. By using our service, you acknowledge that:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number is processed by WhatsApp (Meta) in order to deliver the message to you, as with any WhatsApp message
  • Meta's own privacy policy applies to WhatsApp message delivery
  • Delivery uses an unofficial WhatsApp client rather than the official Business API, which is not endorsed by Meta
  • We only initiate WhatsApp messages after you send us your activation code (you message us first)
  • We do not store your WhatsApp number after your video is delivered

🌐 Third Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare R2 — Temporary video storage (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy)
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — Message and video delivery (facebook.com/privacy/policy)
  • Microsoft Azure — Server hosting (privacy.microsoft.com)
  • Vercel — Website hosting (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Google Analytics — Active on this site for audience and usage measurement (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google AdSense — Will provide advertising when monetization is enabled. Google and its advertising partners may then use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads (policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

🍪 Cookies, Local Storage & Advertising

StatusDrop uses local storage for simple preferences such as remembering that you dismissed a notice. Google Analytics is active and may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions.

When Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, personalize, limit, and measure ads. Before serving ads to visitors in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, StatusDrop will use a Google-certified consent management platform so those visitors can accept, reject, or manage their choices.

You can also control cookies in your browser, manage Google advertising choices at My Ad Center, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used our service, please contact us immediately.

✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect (described in this policy)
  • Request deletion of your data (though it auto-deletes in 5 minutes)
  • Use consent controls, when shown, to accept, reject, or manage analytics and advertising choices
  • Change cookie and advertising choices through your browser or Google settings
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
